## The design (decided; say in the report where it was wrong)
1. **One clip per day, one manifest for the set.** Ten days in one 4k clip is ~350 MB and a 1,440-frame seek
   range; a month would be 1 GB. Instead: `clouds_<date>.mp4` (4k and 2k) per day, exactly today's encode
   (opacity + flow in chroma + height rows + strip), plus `clouds.json` listing days in order with per-day frame
   lists, sizes, seams and held counts. The page keeps TWO `<video>` elements: the playing day and the next day
   preloaded (`preload=auto`, started muted and paused), and swaps at the day boundary on the frame the clock
   crosses midnight, copying the last frame of one and the first of the next into the A/B targets so the warp
   crosses midnight without a hitch. Scrubbing to another day swaps at once and shows the poster of that day
   (`clouds_<date>_poster.webp`) until its first frame presents. The scrubber spans all days; the clock shows
   the date. `fetch_clouds.py encode` gets `--per-day`; keep the single-clip path for one day.
2. **Tiles per day.** `cloud_tiles.py encode` writes `tiles/<date>/L5/`, `tiles/<date>/L4/` and
   `tiles/<date>/L5.json`; `tiles.js` picks the index by the clock's date and keeps decoders across the day
   boundary by resetting to the new day's keyframe 0 (same restart path as a seek). Per-day indexes are loaded
   lazily: only the current day and the next.
3. **Hosting.** Ten days x five satellites of tiles is ~10–15 GB; Vercel static is wrong for that. Cloudflare R2
   bucket `earthai-clouds` (exists, empty) with public access or a Worker in front, Range supported. `wrangler` on
   the laptop is logged in (OAuth), so upload with a script that walks `site/globe/tiles/` and `data/clouds/tenday/`
   and `wrangler r2 object put`s each file (thousands of files: run it under tmux with a manifest of what is done,
   resumable). Layout: `tiles/<sat>/<date>/L<n>/<tx>_<ty>.h264`, `tiles/<sat>/<date>/L<n>.json`,
   `clips/<date>/clouds.mp4` etc. The page reads a base URL from the manifest (`assets: "https://..."`), falling
   back to relative paths, so the one-day site keeps working from Vercel. Keep the 12 Sep clips and tiles on
   Vercel as the fallback. If public R2 needs a custom domain or a token Derek must create, STOP on that step,
   say exactly what he must click, and ship the days that fit on Vercel (the clips alone, ~35 MB a day, do fit).
4. **The page copy** says how many days, which, and what is still missing (a day whose tiles are not built is
   still shown at 10 km). The claim line already switches to "N days".
