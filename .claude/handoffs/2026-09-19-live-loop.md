## Design (decided)
1. **One uniform lag.** Every frame the globe shows is for time T = now − 75 min (Meteosat's licence
   floor with margin; NOAA/JAXA could be fresher but one consistent time per frame matters more).
   A slot is "due" at T + 75 min; the loop fetches the newest due slot from each source, retries
   missed slots for up to 6 h, then marks them held (previous picture, as the pipeline already does).
2. **Catch-up first.** Backfill 16 Sep 00:00Z → now from the official sources at the 4096 grid with
   products (the ten-day set ends 15 Sep 23:50Z), day by day, oldest first, so the page grows a day at
   a time. Tiles for the catch-up days come after the base clips.
3. **The loop.** A `systemd` timer every 10 min runs one idempotent script (`scripts/live_tick.py`):
   fetch due slots → products → opacity/blend/height for those slots (rolling clear-sky reference =
   last 10 days, refreshed once a day at 00:30Z) → append frames to today's day → re-encode today's
   4k/2k clips (≤144 frames, under a minute) and the flow → update `clouds.json` (a `live` block:
   newest frame time, next due, per-source last slot, held counts) → upload changed files to R2
   → bump the manifest on Vercel only when a new day starts (the page reads clips from R2, so the
   manifest on Vercel should point at R2 for clips too; move them there if not already).
   Concurrency lock so ticks never overlap; a tick that runs long logs it and exits.
4. **Tiles, live.** Tile streams are raw Annex B with a NAL index, GOP 12 = 2 h of frames. Encode
   each completed GOP for today and APPEND to the day's tile files + index (no re-encode of the
   whole day); the partial GOP at the end is not served. Level 5 for all five satellites once the
   per-slot budget allows; measure a tick's wall-clock and CPU and report; if a full tick exceeds
   6 min on this box, do level 4 live and level 5 at end of day, and say so.
5. **Retention.** Derived products forever (~2 GB/day; R2). Raw source files deleted 48 h after
   their products exist. Native per-satellite caches for the tile build kept 3 days. Disk watch:
   refuse to fetch when the volume is < 40 GB free and say so in status.
6. **Status and honesty.** `status.json` (in R2, next to the manifest): per-source last successful
   slot, held slots in the last 24 h, last tick duration, disk free, errors of the last tick. The page:
   a small "as of 17:40 UTC · 1 h 12 min ago · next in 6 min" line; a banner when the newest frame is
   > 3 h old ("the feed has stalled; last picture 04:10 UTC"); zone-level note when one source is held
   > 2 h. The default view on load is the newest frame, playing the last 24 h on a loop; the ten-day
   scrubber stays.
7. **Watchdog.** On the box, a second timer every 30 min checks `status.json` age and the newest
   frame age; on stall it restarts the tick service once, and writes `ALERT` into status so the page
   banner shows. No external alerting service (no new accounts); note in the report what a free
   Cloudflare Worker cron + email would take, do not build it.
8. **Cost visibility.** Relabel the box `lease-until=2026-12-31T23:59:00Z owner=earthai-live` (the
   sourcelibrary watchdog stops expired leases; a standing service must not expire). Put the monthly
   estimate in the report. Migration to Hetzner is a follow-up handoff; write `scripts/live_install.sh`
   so the whole service (venv, timers, secrets path, R2 config) installs on a fresh Ubuntu box in one
   command, and test it by re-running it on this box.
