## 2. Model choice for smooth, controllable latent walks

**GAN vs diffusion for this goal.** The literature is blunt: "unlike GANs that have a meaningful compact latent space, the latent space of diffusion models is a noise map that lacks semantic meaning, thus random and abrupt content flickering are often observed when naively interpolating" (DiffMorpher, https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.07409). Fixes exist — Smooth Diffusion (https://github.com/SHI-Labs/Smooth-Diffusion), DiffMorpher's LoRA interpolation, slerp of endpoint noise — but they are per-pair procedures, not a navigable space. GAN direction-finding tools (GANSpace PCA, SeFa closed-form) remain standard in 2025 (https://arxiv.org/html/2305.14551). For "find semantic directions and animate," a StyleGAN-family W space is still the right object.

**Candidates and MPS status:**
- **StyleGAN2-ADA-pytorch (NVlabs):** needs CUDA toolkit per README, but the ops are written with a pure-PyTorch fallback: `if impl=='cuda' and x.device.type=='cuda' and _init(): …cuda… ; return _bias_act_ref(...)` — non-CUDA tensors always take the reference path (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NVlabs/stylegan2-ada-pytorch/main/torch_utils/ops/bias_act.py). The M1 issue is open with no answer (https://github.com/NVlabs/stylegan2-ada-pytorch/issues/230). Expect other CUDA-isms (`torch.cuda` calls, grid-sample grads) to need patching. V100 reference speed: 256² single GPU 21.6–24.2 s/kimg (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NVlabs/stylegan2-ada-pytorch/main/README.md). NVIDIA Source Code License (non-commercial).
- **StyleGAN3:** solves texture-sticking, which is precisely the artefact that ruins GAN morph videos (https://nvlabs.github.io/stylegan3/), but is ~3.6× slower than StyleGAN2 at 256² (13.04 vs 3.61 s/kimg on 8×V100, https://github.com/NVlabs/stylegan3/blob/main/docs/configs.md) and is "heavily" dependent on NVCC-compiled ops; same ref-fallback pattern. A CPU port exists (franknoh/stylegan3-cpu) but the repo 404'd for me — unverified.
- **R3GAN (NeurIPS 2024):** modern ResNet-style G/D, beats StyleGAN2 FID, MIT-free of StyleGAN tricks, but `FusedOperators.py` and `Resamplers.py` import `torch_utils.ops.bias_act/upfirdn2d` with reference classes present and the CUDA variants aliased as default — a two-line swap to `*Reference` makes it plain PyTorch (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/brownvc/R3GAN/main/R3GAN/Resamplers.py). Caveat: all paper configs use 8–64 GPUs, batch 256–4096; no small-data results, though a 2025 medical paper adapts FFHQ-256 to small datasets (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.26828). No license file found.
- **Projected GAN:** MIT; its **FastGAN generator option has no custom kernels** (only the StyleGAN2 G needs CUDA), has a pretrained "landscapes" model, and FastGAN-Lite targets small sets (https://github.com/autonomousvision/projected-gan). Downside for you: FastGAN has no mapping network, so its latent space is less disentangled than W.
- **lucidrains stylegan2-pytorch / lightweight-gan:** pure PyTorch, `--generate-interpolation` built in, "512² flowers after 12 h on 1 GPU" (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lucidrains/lightweight-gan/main/README.md); no MPS mention, but nothing CUDA-specific beyond `.cuda()` calls. Quality trails NVlabs; no ADA-style augmentation in stylegan2-pytorch (it has DiffAugment).
- **StyleGAN-XL/-T:** CUDA 11.1 + custom kernels, large-data oriented (https://github.com/autonomousvision/stylegan-xl). Skip.
- **Diffusion fine-tune (SD/DiT):** diffusers trains on MPS via accelerate; reports range from SDXL LoRA at 10 min/step on M3 Max to 4 s/step for Qwen-Image on M3 Ultra (https://christinasouch.com/blog/qwen-image-lora-training-on-apple-silicon-it-works-and-heres-how). 

**Apple Silicon reality:** the only systematic study found M2 Ultra/Max ~3–4× slower than similarly priced NVIDIA cards, FP16 gives only 20–30 % on Metal, and performance collapses near memory capacity (https://arxiv.org/html/2501.14925). No one has published StyleGAN kimg rates on any M-series chip — unverified gap. My estimate (flagged as estimate): StyleGAN2 256² on M5 Pro via reference ops ≈ 100–250 s/kimg, i.e. 5000 kimg ("already quite reasonable" per NVlabs) ≈ 6–14 days; 50k tiles at 256² is a 2–4-week project without transfer learning.

**Recommendation.** Train a StyleGAN2-class model (for W-space directions) with ADA and 256² first, on the NVlabs stylegan2-ada-pytorch codebase patched for MPS (force `impl='ref'`, replace `torch.cuda` calls), or R3GAN with its Reference ops if you want a cleaner modern architecture and are willing to tune small-batch hyperparameters yourself. Use lucidrains lightweight-gan for a same-day sanity check of the dataset. Avoid StyleGAN3 on MPS (3.6× cost) unless texture-sticking proves visible in your morphs. Keep diffusion for Q3, not for the latent space.

## 3. Upscaling 256/512 → 1K–4K

- **Real-ESRGAN:** BSD-3; PyTorch path defaults to CPU on Macs, MPS patch proposed but unmerged (https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN/issues/902); the **realesrgan-ncnn-vulkan macOS binary** runs on the Apple GPU via MoltenVK and is the practical choice; has video script but no temporal model (https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN).
- **SwinIR / HAT:** plain PyTorch transformers, should run on MPS; HAT beats SwinIR by ~0.5 dB (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.05239). Deterministic → less flicker than diffusion upscalers.
- **SD x4 upscaler:** ~8 GB, 512-crop trained, tiling needed above ~2 MP; runs in diffusers on MPS (https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-diffusion-x4-upscaler). Stochastic per frame → flicker unless seed fixed and noise shared.
- **StableSR:** SD2.1, 128→512, 10–18 GB, NTU S-Lab license, no MPS mention (https://github.com/IceClear/StableSR). **SUPIR:** SDXL, 12 GB+, non-commercial, 10–50× slower (https://github.com/Fanghua-Yu/SUPIR).
- **Satellite-specific:** ESA OpenSR `opensr-model` (LDSR-S2, Sentinel-2 RGB-NIR 10 m→2.5 m, ×4, pip-installable, CPU/GPU only in code; https://github.com/ESAOpenSR/opensr-model); SEN2SR and SEN2NAIP v2 dataset (https://opensr.eu/); S2DR3 (1 m output) is Colab-only/closed (https://www.geodose.com/2025/07/sentinel-2-super-resolution-sr4rs-s2dr3.html). These hallucinate plausible sub-10 m texture — good for your purpose if trained on RGB.
- **Video (temporal) upscalers:** SeedVR2 (ByteDance) has a ComfyUI node with "full MPS support", 3B/7B, GGUF builds for 8–16 GB, batches of 4n+1 frames for coherence, Apache-2.0 (https://github.com/numz/ComfyUI-SeedVR2_VideoUpscaler). FlashVSR is A100-tuned block-sparse attention (https://github.com/OpenImagingLab/FlashVSR); Upscale-A-Video is S-Lab licensed (https://github.com/sczhou/Upscale-A-Video).
- **Native higher-res GAN:** 512² costs ~3× 256² per kimg and 1024² ~7× on V100 tables above — on MPS that is months.

**Recommendation.** Train at 256², render the walk at 256, then upscale as *video*, not frames: SeedVR2-3B (MPS) for 4× to 1024, then Real-ESRGAN ncnn-vulkan or HAT ×2–4 for 4K print. For stills, try opensr-model first (it is literally trained for Sentinel-2-looking input) and HAT as the deterministic fallback. Avoid per-frame SD/StableSR/SUPIR for animation.

## 4. Data

- **EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless:** WMTS/WMS free for non-commercial with attribution; 2018–2024 layers CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, 2016–17 CC BY-SA 4.0; commercial via "EOX Commercial Attribution-RestrictedUse 1.1" (https://eox.at/2025/03/sentinel-2-cloudless-2024/). Original RGB GeoTIFFs: S3 `eox-s2maps` (eu-central-1), **requester-pays**, 512² tiles, zoom 7–13 with z13 ≈ 10 m (https://eox.at/2017/03/sentinel-2-cloudless-original-tiles-available/). No published rate limit for the WMTS; 50k z13 tiles is polite-scraping territory — flag that the NC clause binds derived weights/prints if you ever sell them.
- **Major TOM Core-S2L2A (ESA Φ-lab, HF):** 2.25M patches of 1068² px at 10 m, 12 bands + SEnSeI cloud mask + RGB PNG thumbnail, 23 TB Parquet, CC BY-SA 4.0 — the cleanest "bulk, ML-ready, permissive" option; pull a few parquet shards and crop (https://huggingface.co/datasets/Major-TOM/Core-S2L2A).
- **AWS Earth Search (Element84) sentinel-2-l2a COGs:** free, no account/quota, us-west-2 (https://registry.opendata.aws/sentinel-2-l2a-cogs/ ; API https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1/). Requires your own cloud filtering and compositing.
- **Microsoft Planetary Computer:** Hub retired June 2024, STAC API + data unchanged and free; blob access needs SAS tokens, "rate limiting…to limit egress" unquantified (https://planetarycomputer.microsoft.com/docs/concepts/sas/ ; https://github.com/microsoft/PlanetaryComputer/discussions/347).
- **Copernicus Data Space:** free tier 12 TB/month, 4 concurrent connections, 20 MB/s each, 2,000 S3 requests/month; throttles to 1 MB/s over quota (https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/Quotas.html).
- **NASA GIBS:** public-domain imagery, MODIS-class resolution (250 m+), bulk = >1M tiles/24 h, coordinate 48 h ahead (https://nasa-gibs.github.io/gibs-api-docs/access-advanced-topics/). Too coarse for landforms except as a 3rd scale.
- **Ready-made:** EuroSAT 27k × 64² at 10 m, MIT (https://github.com/phelber/EuroSAT) — too small for 256² output. BigEarthNet v2 549,488 S1/S2 pairs, 10 European countries, CDLA-Permissive-1.0, ~59 GiB S2 (https://bigearth.net/); patches are 120² at 10 m (from v1 spec — unverified for v2). SEN12MS 180,662 triplets at 10 m, CC BY 4.0, 256² patches per paper (https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.07789). fMoW >1M images 0.3–1.5 m, custom "fMoW Challenge Public License" (https://github.com/fMoW/dataset). Million-AID 1,000,848 Google Earth scenes, CC BY-NC-ND (derivatives prohibited — unusable for training outputs). SpaceNet 30 cm WorldView-3, CC BY-SA 4.0, urban only.
- **Avoid:** Google Map Tiles API forbids ML training, caching and non-visualization use (https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/tile/policies); Esri World Imagery (Ward's source) has similar caching restrictions — check https://www.esri.com/en-us/legal/terms/web-site-service before copying his approach (not verified in detail).

**Recommendation.** Use Major TOM Core-S2L2A thumbnails/bands as the primary source: already cloud-masked, global, CC BY-SA, and ~50k 256² crops is under 100 GB of parquet. Stratify by landform using the Core-DEM companion set (https://huggingface.co/datasets/Major-TOM/Core-DEM) plus simple NDWI/NDVI for coasts/deltas/farmland. Supplement with EOX z13 tiles only if you want the pre-composited "pretty" look and accept the NC licence. Skip CDSE and GIBS for this project.
